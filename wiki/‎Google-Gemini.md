# AI Second Brain Audit Report
**Source:** [[‎Google Gemini.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Andrej Karpathy

## Summary
The objective of this audit is to evaluate the alignment of the given repository with [[Andrej Karpathy's AI Second Brain]] / [[AI Knowledge Base]] concepts, using his public [[GitHub Gist]] (https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) as the reference architecture. The audit will inspect the repository and its [[GitHub Wiki]] structure, determining the implementation status of key features such as [[Automatic Knowledge Ingestion]], [[Markdown Knowledge Base]], [[Bidirectional Linking]], and [[Knowledge Graph Generation]].

## Key Takeaways
* The audit will evaluate the implementation status of [[Automatic Knowledge Ingestion]] & [[Deduplication]], [[GitHub Wiki]]-compatible [[Markdown Knowledge Base]], [[Bidirectional Linking]] & [[Cross-Reference Generation]], [[Knowledge Graph Generation]] & [[Semantic Retrieval]], [[Hierarchical Indexes]] & [[Incremental Indexing]], [[Local-First Workflow]], [[AI-Assisted Note Enrichment]] & [[Agent Memory]], and [[Source Attribution]] & [[Search/Retrieval Mechanics]].
* The audit will identify and list occurrences of [[Broken Links]] or missing [[Backlinks]], [[Orphan Notes]] or isolated [[Graph Clusters]], [[Duplicate Notes]], and empty [[Indexes]] or knowledge unreachable from the main [[Index]].
* The audit will evaluate the system's graph based on [[Density & Connectivity]], [[Largest Connected Component]], [[Number of Orphan Nodes]], and [[Link & Semantic Quality]].
* The audit will provide a score for the following categories: [[Architecture]], [[Knowledge Ingestion]], [[Knowledge Graph]], [[Retrieval]], [[Automation]], [[Memory]], and [[Overall]].

## Key Concepts
* [[Andrej Karpathy's AI Second Brain]]: a concept for building a personal knowledge base using [[AI]] and [[GitHub Wiki]].
* [[GitHub Gist]]: a web-based platform for sharing code and other text-based content, used as the reference architecture for this audit.
* [[Automatic Knowledge Ingestion]]: the process of automatically adding new knowledge to the system.
* [[Markdown Knowledge Base]]: a knowledge base built using [[Markdown]] files.
* [[Bidirectional Linking]]: the process of creating links between related pieces of knowledge.
* [[Knowledge Graph Generation]]: the process of generating a graph representation of the knowledge base.
* [[Semantic Retrieval]]: the process of retrieving knowledge based on its meaning.
