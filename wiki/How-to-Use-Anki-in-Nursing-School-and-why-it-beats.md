# How to Use Anki in Nursing School (and why it beats Quizlet)
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-How-to-Use-Anki-in-Nursing-School-and-why-it-beats.md]]
**Channel / Author:** RN Academy

## Summary
Using [[Anki]] in [[Nursing School]] can be a game-changer for students looking to improve their studying efficiency. By leveraging [[Spaced Repetition]] and [[Active Recall]], students can create digital flashcards that help them retain information better than traditional methods like [[Quizlet]]. With [[Anki]], students can create customized decks, add hints, and track their progress, making it an ideal tool for nursing students who need to memorize complex information.

## Key Takeaways
* [[Anki]] is a powerful tool for creating digital flashcards that use [[Spaced Repetition]] and [[Active Recall]] to help students retain information.
* To get started with [[Anki]], students need to download and install the app, create an account, and sync their decks across devices.
* Students can create customized decks using [[Close Deletion Cards]], which allow them to fill in the blank and test their knowledge.
* [[Anki]] allows students to add hints and extra information to their cards, making it easier to review and retain complex information.
* Students can use [[Anki]] on their desktop, mobile device, or through the web app, making it easy to study on-the-go.
* [[Anki]] is particularly useful for nursing students who need to memorize complex information, such as pharmacology and medical terminology.
* By using [[Anki]], students can reduce their study time and improve their retention of information, making it a valuable tool for nursing students.

## Key Concepts
* [[Spaced Repetition]]: a technique used by [[Anki]] to review material at increasingly longer intervals to help solidify it in long-term memory.
* [[Active Recall]]: the process of actively recalling information from memory, rather than simply re-reading it.
* [[Close Deletion Cards]]: a type of card used in [[Anki]] that allows students to fill in the blank and test their knowledge.
* [[Anki Web]]: a web-based version of [[Anki]] that allows students to access their decks and study online.
* [[Anki Droid]]: a mobile app version of [[Anki]] for Android devices.
* [[Quizlet]]: a popular study app that uses digital flashcards, but lacks the advanced features and customization of [[Anki]].
