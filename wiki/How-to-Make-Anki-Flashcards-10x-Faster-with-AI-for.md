# How to Make Anki Flashcards 10x Faster with AI (for free!)
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-How-to-Make-Anki-Flashcards-10x-Faster-with-AI-for.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Ray Amjad

## Summary
The video discusses how to use [[AI]] to automate the process of making [[Anki]] flashcards, allowing users to focus more on understanding the material. The process involves using [[Google AI Studio]] and [[Google Gemini]] to generate flashcards from videos or [[PDF]] documents. The user can then import these flashcards into [[Anki]] and review them, adding images or diagrams as needed to enhance their understanding. This method can be particularly useful for learning complex topics such as [[LLM]] (Large Language Models) or [[Quantum Field Theory]].

## Key Takeaways
* Use [[Google AI Studio]] and [[Google Gemini]] to generate flashcards from videos or [[PDF]] documents
* Import the generated flashcards into [[Anki]] using a [[txt]] file and the pipe separator
* Use the [[Gemini]] prompt to specify the type of flashcards to generate, such as question-answer cards or cloze cards
* Review the generated flashcards and add images or diagrams as needed to enhance understanding
* Use the timestamp feature to link flashcards to specific points in the video or [[PDF]] document
* Consider using a semicolon separator instead of a pipe separator for math-heavy topics to avoid conflicts with equation notation
* The process can be used with videos not on [[YouTube]] by uploading them to [[Google AI Studio]]
* The process can also be used with [[PDF]] documents, such as lecture notes or handouts

## Key Concepts
* [[Anki]]: a flashcard software that uses spaced repetition to help users memorize information
* [[AI]]: artificial intelligence, used in this context to generate flashcards from videos or [[PDF]] documents
* [[Google AI Studio]]: a platform that allows users to access and use [[Google Gemini]] and other AI models
* [[Google Gemini]]: a AI model that can generate flashcards from videos or [[PDF]] documents
* [[PDF]]: a file format used for documents, such as lecture notes or handouts
* [[LLM]]: Large Language Models, a type of AI model that can process and generate human-like language
* [[Quantum Field Theory]]: a branch of physics that studies the behavior of subatomic particles and fields
