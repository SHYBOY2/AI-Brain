## Key Concepts
* **Stock Market**: A market where companies' shares are bought and sold.
* **Options Market**: A market where contracts are bought and sold, giving the buyer the right to buy or sell an underlying asset at a specified price.
* **Forex Market**: A global market where currencies are traded.
* **Crypto Market**: A market where cryptocurrencies, such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, are traded.
* **Trading Plan**: A strategy outlining the trader's goals, risk management, and entry and exit points.
* **Psychological Aspects of Trading**: The emotional and mental factors that can affect a trader's decision-making and performance.
