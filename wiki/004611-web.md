# YouTube Terms and Policies
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-004611-web.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Google LLC

## Summary
The [[YouTube]] platform is governed by a set of [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] that outline the rules and guidelines for users, creators, and advertisers. These [[Policies]] cover aspects such as [[Copyright]], [[Privacy]], and [[Safety]], ensuring a secure and respectful environment for all users. The [[Terms]] of service are established by [[Google LLC]], the parent company of [[YouTube]]. By using the platform, users agree to abide by these [[Terms]] and [[Policies]].

## Key Takeaways
* [[YouTube]] has a comprehensive set of [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] that apply to all users.
* The [[Terms]] of service cover aspects such as [[User Agreement]], [[Community Guidelines]], and [[Copyright]] infringement.
* [[Privacy]] and [[Safety]] are top priorities, with [[Policies]] in place to protect user data and prevent harmful content.
* [[Google LLC]] is responsible for establishing and enforcing the [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] on [[YouTube]].
* Users can access the [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] through the [[YouTube]] website or mobile app.
* [[Creators]] and [[Advertisers]] must comply with the [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] to maintain their presence on the platform.
* [[Developers]] can find resources and guidelines for building [[YouTube]]-integrated applications.
* The [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] are subject to change, and users are encouraged to review them regularly.

## Key Concepts
* [[Terms]]: The rules and guidelines that govern the use of [[YouTube]].
* [[Policies]]: The principles and standards that outline the expected behavior of users, creators, and advertisers on [[YouTube]].
* [[Copyright]]: The legal protection of original content, such as videos, music, and artwork, on [[YouTube]].
* [[Privacy]]: The protection of user data and personal information on [[YouTube]].
* [[Safety]]: The measures in place to prevent harmful or inappropriate content on [[YouTube]].
