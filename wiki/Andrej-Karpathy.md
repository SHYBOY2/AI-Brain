# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Former Director of AI at Tesla and founding member of OpenAI. Known for making complex AI concepts accessible through teaching and writing.

## Relevance to This Wiki

Author of the foundational LLM Wiki gist that this Second Brain is built on. The pattern Karpathy described — using an LLM to incrementally build and maintain a persistent wiki — is the operating model of this entire vault.
