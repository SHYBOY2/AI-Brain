# This Weird Habit Will Make You Unbreakably Disciplined

## Summary
The video discusses a simple yet effective habit to develop unbreakable discipline using a rubber band. By snapping the rubber band whenever procrastination or excuses arise, individuals can interrupt their automatic negative thought loops and take immediate action. This habit helps build self-trust and increases the likelihood of taking action in the future. The concept is based on the idea that the brain tracks every promise made and the ability to follow through, influencing self-trust and discipline.

## Key Takeaways
* The rubber band habit involves snapping a rubber band against the skin to interrupt negative thought patterns and procrastination.
* The brain has an "integrity monitor" that tracks every promise made and the ability to follow through, affecting self-trust and discipline.
