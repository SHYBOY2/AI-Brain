## Key Takeaways
* The stock market is a platform where individuals can buy and sell shares of companies.
* There are three types of traders: retail traders, HNI (High Net Worth Individuals) traders, and institutional traders.
* Retail traders are individuals who trade with their own money, while HNI traders and institutional traders have more resources and expertise.
* Fundamental analysis involves studying a company's financial statements, management, and industry trends to make informed investment decisions.
* Technical analysis involves studying charts and patterns to predict future price movements.

## Key Concepts
* **Stock Market**: A platform where individuals can buy and sell shares of companies.
* **Retail Traders**: Individuals who trade with their own money.
* **HNI Traders**: High Net Worth Individuals who have more resources and expertise.
* **Institutional Traders**: Organizations that trade on behalf of their clients.
* **Fundamental Analysis**: Studying a company's financial statements, management, and industry trends to make informed investment decisions.
* **Technical Analysis**: Studying charts and patterns to predict future price movements.
* **Trading**: Buying and selling shares with the goal of earning a profit.
* **Investing**: Holding shares for a longer period with the goal of earning a return.
