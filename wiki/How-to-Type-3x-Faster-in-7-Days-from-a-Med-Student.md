# How to Type 3x Faster in 7 Days
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-How-to-Type-3x-Faster-in-7-Days-from-a-Med-Student.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Dr Salim Ahmed

## Summary
To type faster, it's essential to focus on [[short burst typing speed]] and [[typing by grouping syllables]] together. This approach, combined with practicing [[touch typing]] and using websites like [[Type Racer]] and [[Monkey Type]], can significantly improve [[typing speed]] and [[productivity]]. Additionally, prioritizing [[accuracy]] and using [[keyboard shortcuts]] can help reduce mistakes and increase efficiency. By implementing these strategies, individuals can become more productive and efficient in their work, such as [[medical school]] or [[university]] studies.

## Key Takeaways
* Improve [[short burst typing speed]] by doing 15-second typing tests 10 times a day using websites like [[Monkey Type]].
* Type by grouping [[syllables]] together as a single motion with your fingers to increase [[typing speed]].
* Practice [[touch typing]] without looking at the keyboard to improve [[productivity]].
* Use websites like [[Type Racer]] to practice typing real sentences and compete with others at a similar level.
* Prioritize [[accuracy]] when typing, especially with complex terminology like [[scientific terminology]].
* Use [[keyboard shortcuts]] like deleting a word at a time with [[option]] and [[backspace]] to increase efficiency.
* Slow down and focus on typing difficult words correctly to develop [[muscle memory]] and improve [[typing speed]].

## Key Concepts
* [[Short burst typing speed]]: The ability to type quickly in short bursts, typically 15 seconds or less.
* [[Typing by grouping syllables]]: The technique of typing by grouping syllables together as a single motion with your fingers.
* [[Touch typing]]: The ability to type without looking at the keyboard, using muscle memory to guide your fingers.
* [[Type Racer]]: A website that allows users to practice typing real sentences and compete with others at a similar level.
* [[Monkey Type]]: A website that provides typing tests and exercises to improve [[typing speed]] and [[accuracy]].
* [[Keyboard shortcuts]]: Pre-defined combinations of keys that perform specific actions, such as deleting a word or sentence.
* [[Muscle memory]]: The ability to perform tasks automatically, without conscious thought, through repeated practice and repetition.
