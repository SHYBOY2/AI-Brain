# How I Study SMARTER, Not HARDER (10 Science-Based Tips)
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-How-I-Study-SMARTER-Not-HARDER-10-Science-Based-Ti.md]]
**Channel / Author:** RESPIRE

## Summary
The most effective students use specific [[study habits]] to learn and retain information, including setting aside dedicated time to study, eliminating [[distractions]], and using [[testing]] as a learning tool. By incorporating these habits into their daily routine, students can improve their ability to [[focus]] and [[retain information]]. Additionally, [[teaching others]] and using [[gap effects]] can also enhance the learning process. By understanding the importance of [[motivation]] and having a [[long-term perspective]], students can develop a more effective approach to learning.

## Key Takeaways
* Set aside dedicated time to study, free from [[distractions]] and interruptions
* Use [[testing]] as a learning tool to improve [[retention]] and [[recall]] of information
* Eliminate [[distractions]] and minimize [[multitasking]] to improve [[focus]]
* Break up study sessions into manageable chunks, using [[gap effects]] to enhance learning
* Teach others what you have learned to reinforce your own understanding and develop [[mastery]]
* Develop a [[long-term perspective]] and understand how your studies will impact your future
* Use [[scheduling]] to create a regular study routine and stick to it
* Limit [[rereading]] and instead focus on [[testing]] and [[recalling]] information
* Make an effort to [[apply]] what you have learned to real-world situations
* Understand the importance of [[neuroplasticity]] and how it relates to learning and memory

## Key Concepts
* [[Study habits]]: the routines and practices used by students to learn and retain information
* [[Distractions]]: anything that interrupts or diverts attention away from the task at hand
* [[Testing]]: the process of evaluating one's knowledge or understanding of a subject
* [[Retention]]: the ability to recall and apply learned information over time
* [[Focus]]: the ability to concentrate and direct attention towards a specific task or goal
* [[Gap effects]]: the phenomenon of improved learning and retention through periodic pauses and repetition
* [[Motivation]]: the driving force behind an individual's actions and decisions
* [[Long-term perspective]]: a broad, abstract understanding of how one's actions and decisions will impact their future
* [[Mastery]]: the state of having complete knowledge or control over a subject or skill
* [[Neuroplasticity]]: the brain's ability to reorganize and adapt in response to new experiences and learning
