# YouTube Terms and Policies
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-003540-web.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Google LLC

## Summary
The [[YouTube]] platform is governed by a set of [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] that outline the rules and guidelines for users, [[Creators]], and [[Advertisers]]. These [[Policies]] cover aspects such as [[Privacy]], [[Copyright]], and [[Safety]], ensuring a secure and respectful environment for all users. The [[Terms]] of service are established by [[Google LLC]], the parent company of [[YouTube]]. Understanding these [[Policies]] is crucial for [[Developers]] and [[Creators]] who want to utilize the platform effectively.

## Key Takeaways
* The [[YouTube]] platform has a comprehensive set of [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] that users must adhere to.
* The [[Privacy Policy]] outlines how user data is collected, stored, and used by [[Google LLC]].
* [[Creators]] and [[Advertisers]] must comply with the [[Copyright]] and [[Intellectual Property]] guidelines.
* The [[Safety]] and [[Community Guidelines]] are in place to ensure a respectful and secure environment for all users.
* [[Developers]] can access the [[Developer]] policies and guidelines to create innovative applications and services on the [[YouTube]] platform.
* The [[Contact us]] page provides a way for users to reach out to [[YouTube]] with questions, concerns, or feedback.
* [[Google LLC]] regularly updates the [[Terms]] and [[Policies]] to reflect changes in the platform and user needs.
* Users can review the [[How YouTube works]] section to understand the platform's functionality and features.
* The [[Test new features]] option allows users to try out new and experimental features on the platform.

## Key Concepts
* [[YouTube]]: a video-sharing platform owned by [[Google LLC]].
* [[Terms]]: the rules and guidelines that govern the use of the [[YouTube]] platform.
* [[Policies]]: the set of principles and guidelines that outline the acceptable use of the platform, including [[Privacy]], [[Copyright]], and [[Safety]].
* [[Google LLC]]: the parent company of [[YouTube]], responsible for establishing and enforcing the [[Terms]] and [[Policies]].
* [[Creators]]: individuals or organizations that produce and upload content to the [[YouTube]] platform.
* [[Advertisers]]: individuals or organizations that use the [[YouTube]] platform to advertise their products or services.
* [[Developers]]: individuals or organizations that create applications and services that interact with the [[YouTube]] platform.
