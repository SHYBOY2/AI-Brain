## Key Concepts
* **Dopamine loading**: a technique to improve productivity and focus by managing dopamine levels throughout the day
* **Traditional dopamine patterns**: fluctuations in dopamine levels throughout the day, leading to low dopamine states and decreased productivity
* **Artificial dopamine releases**: activities that create an unnatural contrast and can make it harder to do difficult work, such as watching porn or scrolling on social media
* **Natural dopamine releases**: activities that can be beneficial for motivation, such as exercising or lifting weights
* **Time blocking**: allocating specific times for work, breaks, and leisure activities to stay focused and motivated
* **Contrast principle**: the brain operates on contrast, meaning that high peaks of dopamine and low troughs of dopamine create a natural contrast that can help with motivation and focus
* **Balance**: maintaining a balance between work and leisure activities to avoid burnout and stay motivated
