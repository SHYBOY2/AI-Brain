# Studying 12-14 Hours a Day with the Pomodoro Technique
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-WANT-TO-STUDY-12-14-HOURSDAY-THE-ONLY-TECHNIQUE-TH.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Dr. Aditya Sanjay Gupta

## Summary
To study for 12-14 hours a day, one must employ the [[Pomodoro Technique]], a method that involves breaking down study sessions into manageable blocks with regular breaks. This technique helps to avoid [[Procrastination]] and maintain [[Discipline]], which is more important than [[Motivation]] when it comes to achieving long-term goals, such as cracking entrance examinations like [[AIIMS]] or [[NEET]]. By using the [[Pomodoro Technique]], individuals can ensure they stay focused and avoid burnout.

## Key Takeaways
* The [[Pomodoro Technique]] involves studying in blocks of 30 minutes, followed by a 4-5 minute break.
* After four cycles, a longer break of 15-30 minutes can be taken to relax and recharge.
* [[Discipline]] is more important than [[Motivation]] when it comes to studying for long hours.
* Breaking down study sessions into smaller blocks helps to avoid [[Overwhelm]] and maintain focus.
* Regular breaks help to prevent [[Burnout]] and maintain productivity.
* The [[Pomodoro Technique]] can be used in conjunction with other study techniques, such as [[Active Recall]] and [[Spaced Repetition]].
* Setting clear goals, such as getting into a top college or medical school, can help to maintain [[Motivation]] and direction.
* Using tools, such as a timer or a study app, can help to stay on track and maintain discipline.

## Key Concepts
* [[Pomodoro Technique]]: a study method that involves breaking down study sessions into 30-minute blocks, followed by regular breaks.
* [[Procrastination]]: the habit of delaying or putting off tasks, often due to lack of motivation or discipline.
* [[Discipline]]: the ability to maintain focus and stick to a study schedule, even when motivation is low.
* [[Motivation]]: the drive or inspiration to achieve a goal, often fueled by emotions or desires.
* [[Burnout]]: a state of physical, emotional, or mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress or overwork.
* [[Active Recall]]: a study technique that involves actively recalling information from memory, rather than simply re-reading it.
* [[Spaced Repetition]]: a study technique that involves reviewing material at increasingly longer intervals to help solidify it in long-term memory.
