## Summary
The way we consume information and the sources we expose ourselves to have a profound impact on our thoughts, beliefs, and ultimately, who we become. Our brains are constantly learning and adapting, and the information we repeatedly expose ourselves to becomes flagged as important, shaping our default way of thinking. To take control of our own education and personal growth, we must learn to consciously direct our attention and focus on the information that aligns with our goals and aspirations.

## Key Takeaways
* Our brains are constantly learning and adapting, regardless of the intentionality of the information we consume
* Repetitive exposure to certain information or experiences can shape our thoughts, beliefs, and behaviors
* The quality of our life is determined by the quality of our mind, making it essential to learn how to direct our own education
* Clarity around what we need to learn is more important than simply accumulating more information
* Focusing on who we want to become, rather than just what we need to learn, is crucial for personal growth and creating the life we want

## Key Concepts
Neuroplasticity: the brain's ability to change and adapt over time through repetition and exposure to new information. This concept highlights the importance of being mindful of the information we consume and the experiences we have, as they can shape our thoughts, beliefs, and behaviors. By understanding neuroplasticity, we can take control of our own education and personal growth, and make intentional decisions about the information we expose ourselves to.
