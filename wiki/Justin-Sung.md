# Justin Sung

Learning coach, certified teacher, research author, and former medical doctor. Founder of iCanStudy. Considered a world-renowned expert in self-regulated learning.

## Background

- Former medical doctor
- Guest lecturer on learning skills at Monash University (Master's and PhD students in Education and Medicine)
- 13+ years as a learning coach
- Has coached tens of thousands of learners internationally
- Runs the iCanStudy program and a free weekly newsletter ("Learning Drops")

## Key Ideas

- **Schema building** as the primary goal of all effective learning
- **Mind mapping (correctly executed)** as the single most powerful study technique
- **Cognitive effort = learning quality** — techniques that feel easy are almost always ineffective
- **AI-assisted Feynman technique** as an S-tier upgrade
