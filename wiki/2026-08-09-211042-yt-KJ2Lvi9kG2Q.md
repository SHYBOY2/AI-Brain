## Key Takeaways
* Reading has numerous benefits, including making you smarter, more empathetic, and more self-aware
* Cut the inner monologue that reads out each word in your head to increase reading speed
* Use your finger to scan the text as you read to improve reading speed
* Stop reading what you don't like and move on to something else
* Don't feel obligated to read every word, and it's okay to skip chapters or put down a book if it's not holding your interest
* Schedule your reading time, even if it's just 30 minutes a day
* Consistency is key, and reading multiple books at once can be beneficial
* Don't worry about finishing every book, and calculate your reading potential
* Highlighting and note-taking are not effective for remembering what you read, and implementation is key
* The goal of reading nonfiction is to change your life, become smarter, or improve yourself in some way
