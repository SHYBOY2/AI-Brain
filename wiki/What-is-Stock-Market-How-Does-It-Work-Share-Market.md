# What is Stock Market & How Does It Work?
## Summary
The stock market is a place where shares of publicly listed companies are traded, allowing individuals to buy and sell ownership units of these companies. To understand the stock market, it's essential to learn about its participants, including companies, investors, and regulators. The stock market exists to facilitate the raising of capital for companies and provide a platform for investors to buy and sell shares.

## Key Takeaways
* The stock market is a platform where shares of publicly listed companies are traded.
* Shares or stocks represent units of ownership in a company.
* Companies can raise capital by issuing shares to the public through an initial public offering (IPO).
* The stock market provides a platform for investors to buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies.
* The main participants in the stock market include companies, investors, brokers, and regulators.
* The stock market exists to facilitate the raising of capital for companies and provide a platform for investors to buy and sell shares.
* Companies can list their shares on the stock market to raise capital and provide liquidity to their shareholders.
* Investors can buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies on the stock market.
* The stock market is regulated by regulatory bodies to ensure fair trading practices and protect investor interests.
* The stock market provides a mechanism for companies to raise capital and for investors to participate in the growth and profits of these companies.

## Key Concepts
* **Stock Market**: A platform where shares of publicly listed companies are traded.
* **Shares or Stocks**: Units of ownership in a company.
* **Initial Public Offering (IPO)**: The process by which a company issues shares to the public for the first time.
* **Publicly Listed Company**: A company whose shares are listed on a stock exchange and can be bought and sold by the public.
* **Regulatory Bodies**: Organizations that oversee and regulate the stock market to ensure fair trading practices and protect investor interests.
* **Investors**: Individuals or institutions that buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies.
* **Brokers**: Intermediaries who facilitate the buying and selling of shares on behalf of investors.
