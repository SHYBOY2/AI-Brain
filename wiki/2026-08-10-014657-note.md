## Summary
Active recall is a learning technique that involves actively recalling information from memory rather than simply re-reading it. This method has been shown to be highly effective in retaining information and promoting long-term understanding. By actively recalling information, individuals can strengthen their memory and improve their ability to apply what they have learned.

## Key Takeaways
* Active recall strengthens memory and improves retention
* It promotes long-term understanding and application of learned information
* This technique is more effective than simply re-reading material
* Active recall can be applied to various subjects and topics
* Regular practice of active recall can lead to significant improvements in learning outcomes

## Key Concepts
Active recall involves actively engaging with learned material by recalling key concepts, summarizing information in one's own words, and testing oneself on the material. This can be achieved through various methods such as flashcards, practice quizzes, and self-testing.
