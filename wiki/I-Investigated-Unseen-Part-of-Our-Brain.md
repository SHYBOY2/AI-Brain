# I Investigated Unseen Part of Our Brain!
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-I-Investigated-Unseen-Part-of-Our-Brain.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Arvind Kalia

## Summary
The human [[Brain]] is a complex and mysterious organ, capable of playing tricks on us. Our [[Perception]] of reality can be altered by our [[Brain]]'s ability to [[Edit]] and manipulate information. The concept of an [[Editor]] in our [[Brain]] is introduced, which is responsible for filtering out unnecessary information and creating a coherent narrative of our experiences. The video also touches on the idea of [[Sleep]] and how it affects our [[Brain]]'s functioning, including the process of [[REM Sleep]] and the role of [[Neurotransmitters]] like [[Acetylcholine]].

## Key Takeaways
* Our [[Brain]] has an [[Editor]] that filters out unnecessary information and creates a coherent narrative of our experiences.
* The [[Editor]] can manipulate our [[Perception]] of time, as seen in the example of the clock experiment.
* [[Sleep]] is not just a passive state, but an active process that involves different stages, including [[REM Sleep]].
* During [[REM Sleep]], our [[Brain]] processes and consolidates memories, and can even help us find solutions to problems.
* The concept of [[Neurotransmitters]] like [[Acetylcholine]] is introduced, which play a crucial role in our [[Brain]]'s functioning.
* The story of [[Peter Tripp]] is shared, who stayed awake for 200 hours and experienced hallucinations, demonstrating the importance of [[Sleep]] for our mental health.
* The idea of [[Thalamus]] is introduced, which acts as a gatekeeper for information entering our [[Brain]].
* The concept of [[Nerve Cells]] communicating through [[Chemicals]] is discussed, and how this discovery was made by [[Otto Loewi]].

## Key Concepts
* [[Brain]]: The complex and mysterious organ that controls our thoughts, feelings, and actions.
* [[Editor]]: The part of our [[Brain]] that filters out unnecessary information and creates a coherent narrative of our experiences.
* [[Sleep]]: The state of rest that our [[Brain]] needs to function properly, which involves different stages, including [[REM Sleep]].
* [[REM Sleep]]: The stage of [[Sleep]] where our [[Brain]] processes and consolidates memories, and can even help us find solutions to problems.
* [[Neurotransmitters]]: Chemicals that transmit signals between [[Nerve Cells]], such as [[Acetylcholine]].
* [[Thalamus]]: The part of our [[Brain]] that acts as a gatekeeper for information entering our [[Brain]].
* [[Nerve Cells]]: The building blocks of our [[Brain]] that communicate through [[Chemicals]].
* [[Chemicals]]: The substances that transmit signals between [[Nerve Cells]], such as [[Acetylcholine]].
