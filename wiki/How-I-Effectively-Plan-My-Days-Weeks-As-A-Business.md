# How I Effectively Plan My Days & Weeks As A Business Owner
**Source:** [[2026-08-10-How-I-Effectively-Plan-My-Days-Weeks-As-A-Business.md]]
**Channel / Author:** Ross Harkness

## Summary
To effectively plan days and weeks as a business owner, it's essential to have a clear direction and [[Long-term Goals]] to work towards. This involves setting [[90-day Actionable Goals]], also known as [[Rocks]], which are broken down into smaller, manageable [[Strategic Actions]]. By prioritizing these actions and scheduling them in a [[Notebook]] or planner, business owners can ensure they're focusing on high-leverage activities that drive growth. Additionally, creating a [[Standard Week]] with recurring tasks and delegating non-essential tasks can help streamline the planning process.

## Key Takeaways
* Set [[Long-term Goals]] and break them down into [[90-day Actionable Goals]] or [[Rocks]]
* Create a [[Warm Map]] to visualize and plan [[Strategic Actions]]
* Use a [[Notebook]] or planner to schedule tasks and prioritize [[Strategic Actions]]
* Break down large tasks into smaller, manageable tasks to reduce overwhelm and increase productivity
* Create a [[Standard Week]] with recurring tasks to streamline planning
* Delegate non-essential tasks to free up time for high-leverage activities
* Schedule tasks in order of priority, with the most important task ([[MIT]]) first
* Use the [[Maker/Manager Schedule]] to prioritize high-leverage activities in the morning and low-importance tasks in the afternoon
* Stick to the schedule and avoid distractions to ensure effective planning

## Key Concepts
* [[Long-term Goals]]: The overall objectives a business owner wants to achieve, often set for a period of 1-5 years.
* [[90-day Actionable Goals]] or [[Rocks]]: Specific, achievable goals set for a 90-day period, designed to move the business towards its long-term objectives.
* [[Strategic Actions]]: The specific steps or tasks needed to achieve a [[Rock]] or [[90-day Actionable Goal]].
* [[Notebook]]: A physical or digital notebook used to plan and schedule tasks, often with a custom layout or template.
* [[Standard Week]]: A template or schedule that outlines recurring tasks and activities for each day of the week.
* [[Maker/Manager Schedule]]: A scheduling approach that prioritizes high-leverage activities (making) in the morning and low-importance tasks (managing) in the afternoon.
* [[MIT]] (Most Important Task): The most critical task that needs to be completed, often the one that will have the greatest impact on achieving the business's goals.
