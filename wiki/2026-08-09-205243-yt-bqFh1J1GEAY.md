## Summary
Shadowing is a technique used to improve speaking skills by repeating after a native speaker in real-time. However, many students make a common mistake that hinders their progress, despite being able to understand English at a high level. This mistake can be overcome by using a structured approach to shadowing, which involves three levels of practice: pause, follow, and a third level that allows for more spontaneous speech.

## Key Takeaways
* Shadowing is not just about copying every word, but also about absorbing the speaker's accent, pronunciation, tone, rhythm, and way of speaking.
* Many students struggle to speak fluently despite being able to understand English at a high level, due to a lack of practice in speaking.
* The "shadowing ladder" consists of three levels: pause, follow, and a third level that allows for more spontaneous speech.
* Recording oneself while practicing shadowing can help track progress and identify areas for improvement.
* Using material that is relevant to one's real life can make shadowing more effective and useful.

## Key Concepts
* **Shadowing**: repeating after a native speaker in real-time to improve speaking skills.
* **Pause**: the first level of shadowing, where the speaker pauses the audio to carefully copy the pronunciation of each word.
* **Follow**: the second level of shadowing, where the speaker follows the audio without stopping, focusing on the rhythm and timing of the sentence.
* **Shadowing ladder**: a structured approach to shadowing that consists of three levels of practice.
