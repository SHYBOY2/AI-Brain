# Training the Subconscious Mind
## Summary
The minutes before sleep are crucial in determining how the mind functions for the next 8 hours. By giving clear instructions to the subconscious mind during this time, individuals can influence their mental patterns and behaviors. The subconscious mind is most receptive when the conscious mind is quiet, and it responds to clarity rather than effort or emotional intensity. By training the mind to work with you while you rest, you can achieve consistent results and reduce resistance in life.

## Key Takeaways
* The subconscious mind is most receptive when the conscious mind is quiet, typically before sleep.
* The subconscious mind responds to clear instructions given at the right level, rather than wishes or emotional intensity.
* The mind has different states, including the alpha level, where it stops arguing and executes instructions.
* To train the subconscious mind, lie down comfortably, count slowly backward from 5 to 1, and then formulate a single clear instruction in the present tense.
* The instruction should be simple, precise, and without emotional strain, such as "my mind works to find the best solution."
* Allow yourself to fall asleep without repeating or analyzing the instruction.
* The subconscious mind works best when it is not being watched or rushed, and results may not arrive as expected.
* Consistently training the subconscious mind can lead to reduced resistance in life and improved mental clarity.
* This technique supports conscious action and prepares the ground for the conscious mind to act.
* The goal is to train the mind, not to manifest or force results, and to listen to yourself at the correct level.

## Key Concepts
* **Subconscious mind**: the part of the mind that operates below conscious awareness, responsible for organizing information, reinforcing habits, and processing decisions.
* **Alpha level**: a measurable neurological state where the mind stops arguing and executes instructions.
* **Clear instructions**: simple, precise, and unemotional directives given to the subconscious mind.
* **Conscious mind**: the part of the mind responsible for conscious awareness, analysis, and decision-making.
* **Mental patterns**: habits and behaviors that are influenced by the subconscious mind.
* **Resistance**: the opposition or obstacles that individuals may face in life, which can be reduced by training the subconscious mind.
